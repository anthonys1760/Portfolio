steps:
  - title: Gateway
    body: One process sits between the agent and every MCP server. Stdio in, a namespaced catalog out.
  - title: Policy, taint, wrap
    body: "First-match policy decides the scanner. Untrusted results come back between `<<untrusted>>` and `<<end untrusted>>`, and the session is tainted."
  - title: Approvals
    body: After untrusted content, a send waits for a person. Approve once, or deny. Unanswered calls are denied after 120 seconds.
  - title: Audit chain
    body: Every event is hashed to the one before it. The replay recomputes the chain in your browser, so nothing is trusted just because it is on disk.
